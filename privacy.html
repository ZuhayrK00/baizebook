BaizeBook help

Your table. Your data.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Information stored by the app

BaizeBook stores player names, scores, match settings, frames, breaks and shot histories locally on your device. The developer does not collect this information. There are no accounts, advertisements, tracking services or analytics SDKs.

Apple Watch

The selected frame and player names are transferred between your iPhone and paired Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity. Watch scoring commands return to your iPhone. No developer server is used.

Exports and device backups

If you export or share a backup, the file includes player names and match history. It is sent only to the destination you choose. Apple's device backups may contain app data according to your device and account settings.

Control and removal

You can edit or archive players and export your records. Archiving keeps their history. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data; exported files remain wherever you saved them. Export a backup before reinstalling or changing devices.

External links

The scoring guide may open the WPBSA website. External websites operate under their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the BaizeBook support page.

Changes

If future versions change these practices, this policy and the in-app privacy information will be updated.
