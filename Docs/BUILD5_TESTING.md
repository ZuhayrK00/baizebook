# BaizeBook 1.0 (5) — What to Test

Bottom sheets now fit their content, removing the excess space beneath Cancel or the final action. Check multiple reds, choosing a frame winner, ending a session, deleting a match, fouls, free balls, score adjustments and player editing. Larger text remains scrollable.

Settings → Your data → Clear all data now provides a fresh start after an explicit warning. Cancel keeps your data. Confirming permanently removes players, matches, history and statistics, resets preferences and clears the active match and Watch scoreboard.

Export a backup before testing Clear all data. Existing backup files remain available. After resetting, check that Play, Players and History are empty, relaunch the app, then import your backup to restore it.
