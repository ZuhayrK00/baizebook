# BaizeBook TestFlight

BaizeBook uses a private internal testing group named **BaizeBook Personal Testing**, with automatic distribution enabled. On 5 October 2026, **1.0 (build 5)** was verified as **Testing**. The group shows **1 Tester / 4 Builds**, and the account-holder tester row reports **Installed 1.0 (5)**. Earlier builds remain available as older versions.

## Build 5 update — available in TestFlight

Bottom sheets fit their content, with larger accessibility content remaining scrollable. Settings → Your data → **Clear all data** offers a fresh start with a warning and explicit confirmation. Cancel preserves the data; confirming clears players, matches, preferences and the Watch scoreboard. Export a backup before testing a reset, then import it to restore your history. See `Docs/BUILD5_TESTING.md` for the focused checklist.

After potting a single red, **Potted multiple reds?** appears above Foul / End turn. Select the total reds in that shot, including the first red. Undo and redo apply to the whole shot. Frame winners, ending sessions and deleting matches use rounded sheets with explicit choices and cancellation. SnookerMate import, manual past games, open sessions, simpler fouls, player-name switching, frame stories, history editing and Watch controls are included. All data remains local.

## Install on iPhone

1. Install Apple's TestFlight app from the App Store.
2. Open the BaizeBook invitation sent to the Apple developer account email and accept it on your iPhone.
3. Open BaizeBook in TestFlight and tap Update (or Install if this is your first installation).
4. For wrist scoring, install the included BaizeBook companion on your paired Apple Watch through the Watch app, then open a match on the iPhone.

If the invitation is missing, open the group in App Store Connect and resend the invitation once a build is marked available for testing. The App Store Connect dashboard is a management link, not a public TestFlight installation link.

## Check the update

Import the SnookerMate JSON through Settings → Import games or backup, review the preview and link players before confirming. Re-importing skips existing matches. History → + adds a past game; open a saved frame to correct its scores, winner, date or highest break. Try undo and redo after editing. New Match has an open-session toggle for games without a best-of limit. On the scoring screen, tap either player’s name to switch turns; finish/concede asks who won. Swipe the Watch pages to reach scoring and match controls.

## First device check

Create two players and a one-frame match. Pot a red and black; confirm 8 points, undo to 1, then end the turn. Test a foul and finish a frame. Reopen the app and confirm the match remains saved. Export a backup, import it again, and confirm there are no duplicates. Try scoring from the paired Watch and confirm the phone updates once. Check text size, screen readability and taps at a real table.

Export a backup before removing the app or moving devices. Data stays locally on the phone. The Watch communicates directly with the paired phone; there is no server account.
